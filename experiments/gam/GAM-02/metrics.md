# GAM-02 metrics (src/common/metrics.py)

GAM-01 + 10 pairwise interaction terms (FAST-selected inside the training window) (own calibration: Platt slope from inner B, level = training rate; 'raw' table = EBM probabilities without Platt)

AR = 2*AUC - 1. Brier = overall probability accuracy (not a calibration measure). Calibration: mean_pd vs observed_dr (calibration-in-the-large, exact binomial p-value) and logistic recalibration intercept/slope (perfect: 0 / 1).
