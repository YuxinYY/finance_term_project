# LGB-v4-mono vs LGB-v4-free: recommendation

**Recommendation: keep LGB-v4-mono as the GBM entry; LGB-v4-free is a diagnostic only (GBv4-05, OPEN for team decision).**

- Cost of the constraints (tables below, from `comparison_bootstrap.csv`): free minus mono AUC +0.0022 [+0.0005, +0.0039] on FY2009 and
  +0.0015 [-0.0001, +0.0032] on FY2010; log-loss -0.00025 and -0.00024 (both CIs exclude 0). A real but small cost, the same
  size as the v3 Colab run (free minus mono +0.0009 / +0.0011, bootstrap table in the Colab report) and D-126 (+0.003 to +0.005 on the round-2 feature set).
- What the AUC buys in the free model: wrong-signed effects on ratios with a clear direction. On the 25 features that mono
  constrains, free has 2 / 3 SHAP-direction violations (FY2009 / FY2010: operating_cash_flow_margin, debt_detail_missing;
  int_coverage, quick_ratio, operating_cash_flow_ratio) and 11 / 11 ICE violations. Example: in the FY2010 free model a
  higher interest coverage (EBITDA / financing expense) RAISES the PD on average (Spearman +0.58 between value and SHAP), which a credit officer
  cannot defend. Likely cause: zero financing expense makes int_coverage equal EBITDA in euros (D-154 (3)).
- Mono's remaining flags are all on features deliberately left unconstrained: the raw euro amounts ebitda and exp_financing
  (SHAP) plus profit, prof_operations and lt_debt_to_capital (ICE). The raw amounts proxy size (consistency report M8); the
  ratio versions (ebitda_to_debt, financing_expense_ratio) carry the right sign. lt_debt_to_capital is free on purpose (GBv4-02).
  Every constrained feature has 0% of ICE rows moving against its sign.
- Level (both variants, PROVISIONAL): the prior shift to the validation-year rate leaves FY2009 / FY2010 about 1.2x
  under-predicted (default rates kept rising after the validation year; GBv4-04).
- New firms: AUC drops to about 0.76-0.78 for firms not seen in the training or validation years (`LGB-v4-mono/metrics.md`).

The v3-vs-v4 table at the end quotes other runs whose model settings differ (other hyper-parameters, split design,
calibration, 80 vs 81 features). It is not a test of the split effect.

## Face-validity violations (features with an expected sign; `face_validity.csv`)
