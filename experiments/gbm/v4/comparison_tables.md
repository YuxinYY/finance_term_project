## Face-validity violations (features with an expected sign; `face_validity.csv`)

SHAP violation: Spearman(feature value, TreeSHAP) has |rho| >= 0.05 and the opposite sign to the expected one. ICE violation: for more than 10% of 500 test rows, moving the feature from its 5th to its 95th test-year percentile (others fixed) changes the PD against the expected sign. face_validity.csv also has the strict share (any step against the sign on a 41-point grid).
