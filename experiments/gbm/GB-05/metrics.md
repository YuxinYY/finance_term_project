# GB-05 metrics (src/common/metrics.py)

GB-01 with LightGBM's 'advanced' monotone method (still fully monotone, less restrictive splits)

AR = 2*AUC - 1. Brier = overall probability accuracy (not a calibration measure). Calibration: mean_pd vs observed_dr (calibration-in-the-large, exact binomial p-value) and logistic recalibration intercept/slope (perfect: 0 / 1).
