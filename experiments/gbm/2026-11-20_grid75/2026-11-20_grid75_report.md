## Addendum: robustness of the free-vs-mono gap (`2026-11-20_04_seed_check.py`, `seed_check.csv`)

Grid and refit differ slightly in rounds 2-3 (round 1 identical) because LightGBM builds histogram bins from a
200,000-row sample by default. Re-run of the shared winner setting with bins from all rows and seeds 1-3:
free - mono dAUC by round +0.00019 / +0.00054 / +0.00168 (overall +0.00081; free ahead in 9 of 9 runs);
dLL 0.00000 / -0.00011 / -0.00022 (overall -0.00011, about -0.2%). Best iterations: free 106-215, mono 179-287.
Final fit must set `bin_construct_sample_cnt` >= number of rows (deterministic, full-data bins).

Note on the sign gate: the pre-set rule only checks top-10 drivers. Outside the top 10 the free model moves
against the expected sign on quick_ratio (83% of movement, gain rank 14), int_coverage (75%, rank 22) and
operating_cash_flow_margin (71%, rank 32), so per-firm reason codes for those ratios would be wrong-signed.
