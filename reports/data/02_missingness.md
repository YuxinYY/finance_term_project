## 3. Is missingness structural? (abbreviated statements hypothesis)

Hypothesis H2a: firms below the size limits of Italian Civil Code art. 2435-bis may file an abbreviated balance sheet (bilancio in forma abbreviata), with the balance sheet shown only at aggregated item level (R-27, current text VERIFIED; the limits in force 2007-2012, reported as EUR 4.4M assets / 8.8M revenue / 50 employees from 2008, are SECONDARY only). If abbreviated balance sheets show payables only in total (within / beyond 12 months) [UNVERIFIED detail], the bank/fin/AP split would be missing while `debt_st`/`debt_lt` are present: the pattern in section 2. The tests below check whether the block's missingness falls with size and is persistent within firm. Data alone cannot prove the legal mechanism.

Vintage effect: ST block missing on 16.3% of 2008-2010 rows vs 7.3% of 2007/2011/2012 rows.
