## 5. Accounting identities and how derived fields are defined (Roger G2)

Tolerance: |lhs - rhs| <= 1% of max(|lhs|,|rhs|) or <= EUR 2 (rounding).

Residuals: asset side `asst_tot - components` is >= -2 on 99.90% of rows (median share of assets 0.199%, p99 20.06%): an omitted asset item (accruals/other) makes the sum fall short; components never exceed the total. Liability side residual >= -2 on 99.84% of rows. `debt_st` exceeds bank+fin+AP short-term on 86.3% of testable rows (median gap 22.2% of debt_st): `debt_st` is total short-term liabilities (incl. other payables such as tax and social-security debts [hypothesis]), so the split is a decomposition with a residual, not an identity.

**Field definitions discovered (exact to rounding on >= 99.9% of rows):** `roa` = 100 x prof_operations / asst_tot (an operating ROA in %, NOT net profit / assets); `roe` = 100 x profit / eqty_tot in %; `prof_operations` = rev_operating - COGS (so COGS = all operating costs); `wc_net` = asst_current - debt_st; `margin_fin` = eqty_tot - fixed assets (as Appendix B says); `cf_operations` = profit + (ebitda - prof_operations), i.e. net profit + D&A, an accounting cash-flow proxy, not a cash-flow-statement figure.

`roe` is missing on 72,937 rows: of these, 51,933 have |100 x profit/equity| >= 150 and 38,985 have equity <= 0; observed roe lies in [-150.0, 149.99]. Missing roe with |calc| < 150 and equity > 0: 0. So `roe` is truncated at +/-150 and blanked otherwise: **its missingness encodes extreme ROE or negative equity** and must not be median-imputed; recompute from profit and eqty_tot instead.

`goodwill` is almost certainly mislabelled: positive median EUR 420,702, 777,317 distinct values, Spearman corr with rev_operating 0.83 and with ebitda 0.80, and ebitda <= goodwill on 99.99% of rows. Hypothesis: it is value added (valore aggiunto = EBITDA + personnel costs). Do not interpret it as goodwill in the deck.

`inc_financing` has a negative median (-32,164) and equals -exp_financing on 39.9% of rows; `prof_financing` is non-zero on only 4.2% of rows. Hypothesis: `inc_financing` is the NET financial result and `prof_financing` is a small adjustment item (e.g. value adjustments of financial assets). Use `exp_financing` for interest-burden ratios. `days_rec` does not equal 365 x AR / revenue (match 1.2%), is negative on 74,688 rows and missing on 72.3%: compute days receivable from AR and revenue instead.

**Reconstruction potential** (used in 02_missingness.md): `margin_fin`, `wc_net`, `roa`, `roe`, `prof_operations` are exact functions of other fields; the bank/fin/AP debt split can only be bounded (components <= debt_st/debt_lt), not reconstructed.

## 7. Selection bias (Roger M16): what the data can and cannot tell us

- The file has no loan, application, approval or pricing fields. Appendix B calls the firms 'previous borrowers'; we cannot check that from the data. Rejected applicants are absent by construction (no applicant-level records), so reject inference is impossible.
- Coverage: 144,528-185,639 firms per year, 20 of 20 regions, 83 ATECO divisions. Hypothesis (unverified, needs external firm counts): this looks more like a broad sample of Italian corporations above the asset threshold than one bank's loan book. If so, the 'selection bias from only booked loans' concern in the proposal is weaker than stated; the selection that IS visible is (a) the EUR 1.5MM asset filter applied each year and (b) firms that stop filing before default (00_labels.md section 1).
- Survivorship: firms first appear in 2007 or later and leave when they stop filing, default or fall below the threshold; default_rate per year is therefore a rate among filing firms above the threshold.
