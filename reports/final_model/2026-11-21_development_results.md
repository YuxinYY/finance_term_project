## 1. Setup

- Data: Chloe's 11.20 files, 75 VIF-selected features, 5-month label (D-151); audited clean (D-156).
- Walk-forward rounds (train / validation / test):

| round    | train     |   validation | test           |
|:---------|:----------|-------------:|:---------------|
| round 01 | 2007      |         2008 | 2009           |
| round 02 | 2007-2008 |         2009 | 2010           |
| round 03 | 2007-2009 |         2010 | 2011 (lockbox) |
- Every round: preprocessing and model fitted on train only; early stopping on validation AUC; test years reported only, never used to choose (round-3 test FY2011 is the lockbox).
- Selection rule (D-158, fixed before results): mean validation AUC over the 3 rounds; settings within 0.001 AUC tie; ties go to lower mean validation log loss, then the simpler setting.
- Grid: 110 of 288 settings run in both modes (monotone with the D-157 sign table, unconstrained) x 3 rounds = 660 fits, learning rate 0.05.
